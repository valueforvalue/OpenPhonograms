Atkinson Hyperlegible
====================

Designed by the Braille Institute of America, Inc.

  Project lead: Applied Design Works
  Web:          https://www.brailleinstitute.org/freefont/
  License:      SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see OFL.txt)

The four faces bundled in this directory are:

  AtkinsonHyperlegible-Regular.ttf
  AtkinsonHyperlegible-Italic.ttf
  AtkinsonHyperlegible-Bold.ttf
  AtkinsonHyperlegible-BoldItalic.ttf

This typeface is designed for maximum legibility at low vision. The
letterforms include disambiguated shapes (e.g., capital I / lowercase l /
numeral 1 are distinct) to reduce reading errors for low-vision readers.
