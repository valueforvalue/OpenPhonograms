## Teacher Script

> **Say:** "Today we are going to learn a new phonogram. A phonogram is a letter or group of letters that makes one or more sounds."

<div class="phonogram">{pg}</div>

> **Say:** "This is the phonogram **{pg}**." *(Point to the card.)* "It says {sounds}."

> **Say:** "Listen to me say its sounds: {sounds}. Now you say them."

> *(Child repeats.)*

> **Say:** "Good. Now let's write it. Watch me first." *(Demonstrate writing {pg}.)*

> **Say:** "Now you write **{pg}** three times. Say '{sounds}' each time you write it."

> *(Child writes on whiteboard. Check formation.)*

> **Say:** "Every time you see this phonogram, you will try each of its sounds until the word makes sense. Let's practice with some words."

{rule_script}

> **Say:** "What did you learn today?" *(Child responds: "The phonogram {pg} — it says {sounds}.")*
