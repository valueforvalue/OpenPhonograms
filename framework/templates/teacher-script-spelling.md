## Teacher Script: Spelling Analysis

Follow this script for each spelling word:

> **Say:** "The first word is **{word}**. Listen: *{sentence}*. Now you say the word."

> *(Child repeats the word.)*

> **Say:** "Let's break **{word}** into sounds. Hold up your fingers — one finger for each sound."

> *(Hold up fingers as child segments. Guide if needed.)*

> **Say:** "Now write **{word}**. Sound it out as you write."

> *(Child writes. Watch for correct phonograms.)*

> **Say:** "Let's check your spelling. What phonograms did you use? Underline any multi-letter phonograms. Did any rules apply?"

> *(Child identifies phonograms and rules. Discuss.)*

> **Say:** "Read the word sound by sound, then blend it."

> *(Child reads: /s/ /h/ /ĭ/ /p/ → ship.)*

Repeat for each word.
