## What You're Getting

A complete print-first reading curriculum, organized by stage:

| Stage | Age | Lessons | Focus |
|-------|-----|---------|-------|
| 1 | Pre-K (4-5) | 48 | Phonemic awareness + 26 letter phonograms |
| 2 | Kindergarten (5-6) | 56 | CVC words + 25 multi-letter phonograms + 6 rules |
| 3 | Grade 1 (6-7) | 56 | Silent E (9 reasons) + 17 PGs + 9 rules + syllables |
| 4 | Grade 2 (7-8) | 48 | Schwa, suffixing, Latin /sh/, morphology |
| 5 | Grade 3+ (8+) | 40 | 25 Latin/Greek roots + fluency + composition + grammar |

**Total: 248 lessons** + 178 worksheets + 25 decodable readers + 8 reference aids.

## Quick Start (5 Minutes)

1. **Download the release ZIP** (or build from source — see [docs/BUILD.md](BUILD.md))
2. **Extract** to a folder on your computer
3. **Print the essentials**:
   - Phonogram chart (one for the wall)
   - Spelling rules poster
   - Spelling Analysis routine poster
   - Your stage's first 10 lesson packs
4. **Read** [TEACHER-GUIDE.md](../TEACHER-GUIDE.md) — comprehensive reference
5. **Open lesson pack PDF #1** and start teaching

That's it. Every lesson is self-contained.

### Lesson Pack Contents

Each `lesson-NN-{lesson_id}.pdf` is a **cohesive bundle** containing:

1. **Cover page** — stage, lesson #, new PG/rule, prep checklist
2. **Teacher script** — the lesson to teach
3. **Worksheet** — matched practice page (if applicable)
4. **Flash cards** — for all PGs taught so far in this stage
5. **Reader** — if a reader lesson
6. **Home practice** — to send home

Print one pack per lesson. Everything you need is inside.

## Daily Lesson Workflow

**15-45 minutes per lesson** depending on stage. Structure:

| Time | Activity | What You Need |
|------|----------|---------------|
| 2-5 min | **Phonogram flash review** | Flash cards for all PGs taught so far |
| 5 min | **New concept introduction** | The lesson script (in the pack) |
| 10-15 min | **Spelling Analysis** | Whiteboard + marker, 3-6 words |
| 5 min | **Reading practice** | Worksheet or reader (in the pack) |
| 3 min | **Quick check** | 3 questions at end of lesson script |

### Per-Stage Timing

- **Stage 1** (Pre-K): 15-20 min/lesson, 3-4 lessons/week
- **Stage 2** (K): 25-35 min/lesson, 4-5 lessons/week
- **Stage 3** (Gr 1): 30-40 min/lesson, 4-5 lessons/week
- **Stage 4** (Gr 2): 35-45 min/lesson, 4-5 lessons/week
- **Stage 5** (Gr 3+): 40-50 min/lesson, 4-5 lessons/week

## Stage-by-Stage Guide

### Stage 1: Phonemic Awareness & First Phonograms (48 lessons)

**Before starting:** Child speaks in complete sentences, follows two-step directions, holds a pencil.

**Lesson structure:** Flash review → introduce new PG or play PA game → writing practice → quick check.

**Phonograms taught (groups of 6):**
- Group 1: a, d, g, c, o, qu
- Group 2: s, t, i, p, u, j
- Group 3: r, n, m, e, l, b
- Group 4: h, k, f, v, w, x
- Group 5: y, z

**Plus 8 phonemic-awareness lessons** at the start (no letters — just sounds).

**Mastery check (Lesson 48):** All 26 sounds in 2 seconds, blend/segment CVC, identify initial/final/medial sounds.

### Stage 2: CVC Words & First Multi-Letter Phonograms (56 lessons)

**Before starting:** Stage 1 assessment passed (85%+).

**Phonograms taught:** sh, th, ck, ee, ng, ar, or, er, oi, oy, ai, ay, ch, wh, ea, ow, ou, oo, ed, igh, aw, au, ir, ur, oa, ear (25 multi-letter PGs).

**Rules introduced:** 26 (CK), 3 (No I/U/V/J), 9 (AY at end), 4 (Long at end), 20 (-ED), 28 (GH), 30 (Floss).

**Key new skill:** Spelling Analysis (3-5 words per lesson).

**Mastery check (Lesson 56):** 26 multi-letter PGs, short/long vowels, blends, 6 rules.

### Stage 3: Silent E & Vowel Teams (56 lessons)

**Key new concept:** Say-to-Spell — deliberately mispronouncing a word with clear vowels to hear its spelling. Introduced at Lesson 47 (schwa). Used for all multi-syllable words thereafter.

**Silent E has 9 reasons:**
1. Vowel says long sound (most common)
2. No V or U at end
3. C → /s/, G → /j/
4. Every syllable needs a vowel
5. Keep S from looking plural
6. Make word look bigger
7. TH becomes voiced (/th/ as in "this")
8. Clarify meaning (homophones)
9. Unseen reason (memorize: come, some, one)

**Mastery check (Lesson 56):** All 9 SE reasons, 17 new PGs, syllable division, 9 rules.

### Stage 4: Suffixing, Latin /sh/ & Morphology (48 lessons)

**Key new skills:**
- Schwa identification (every unstressed syllable has a schwa)
- Drop E / double consonant / Y → I rules
- Latin /sh/ phonograms (ti, ci, si) + Rules 17-18
- 4 prefix pairs + 6 suffix pairs

**Mastery check (Lesson 48):** Suffixing, Latin /sh/, morphology, 6 more rules.

### Stage 5: Roots, Fluency & Composition (40 lessons)

**Roots taught:**
- **12 Latin**: dict, duct, spect, port, form, struct, rupt, ject, tract, scrib, mit/miss, vid/vis
- **10 Greek**: graph, phon, bio, geo, therm, meter, scope, auto/tele, micro/chron, photo/logy

**Beyond decoding:** Repeated reading for fluency, sentence building, paragraph composition, parts of speech, punctuation.

**Mastery check (Lesson 40):** 25 roots, fluency (WPM), composition, grammar.

## Spelling Analysis — The Core Routine

Every lesson uses this 5-step routine. Print the poster (`reference/spelling-analysis.html`) and keep it visible.

| Step | Teacher | Student |
|------|---------|---------|
| 1. **Hear & Say** | Say word. Use in sentence. | Repeat. |
| 2. **Segment** | Hold up fingers (1 per sound). | Break word into sounds. |
| 3. **Write** | Watch student write. | Write phonograms. |
| 4. **Analyze** | "What rules? Underline multi-letter PGs." | Identify phonograms + rules. |
| 5. **Read** | "Read it sound by sound, then blend." | Sound out, then blend. |

**Example (Stage 2, Lesson 11 — introducing "sh"):**

Word: **ship**

1. Hear & Say: "ship. The ship sails on the sea." → child repeats
2. Segment: sh-ĭ-p (3 fingers)
3. Write: child writes "ship" while sounding out
4. Analyze: "sh is a multi-letter phonogram that says /sh/. The i is short. p says /p/."
5. Read: "Read it sound by sound." /sh/-/ĭ/-/p/. "Now blend." ship.

**Say-to-Spell (Stage 3+):** For multi-syllable words, first say the word normally, then pronounce with clear vowels (e.g., "about" → "ā-bout"), then segment, write, read normally.

## Web Game

Open `phonogram-trainer.html` in any browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari). Self-contained, no install needed.

**4 modes:**
1. **Flash** — Child sees phonogram, says all sounds within 2 seconds (like flash cards but with audio)
2. **Match** — Match phonograms to pictures/sounds
3. **Speed** — How fast can you identify 10 PGs?
4. **Browse** — See all 75 phonograms at once

**Settings:** Filter by stage (only show PGs the child knows). Audio on/off. Progress tracking in browser localStorage.

Best for: independent practice, reward time, summer review.

## Troubleshooting Common Problems

| Problem | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| Child can't blend CVC | Not enough phonemic awareness | Return to Stage 1 lessons 6-7 (oral blending) |
| Mixes up b/d, p/q | Visual confusion | Separate by 1+ week; use multi-sensory tracing (sand tray, finger on textured surface) |
| Guesses at words instead of sounding out | Sight word habit creeping in | Go back to sound-by-sound; no guessing allowed. Remind: every word can be sounded out. |
| Can't remember phonogram sounds | Not enough flash review | 5 min daily flash drill. Say ALL sounds each time. |
| Spells "mak" for "make" | Silent E not internalized | Return to Stage 3 Lesson 3. Practice the 9 reasons. |
| Says /ā/ for "a" in "cat" | Taught short sound after long | Always teach ALL sounds from the start. Never "a says /ă/" then add more later. |
| Reads robotically (no fluency) | Needs repeated reading | Use Stage 5 fluency passages. Time and re-read. |
| Can't spell multi-syllable words | Not using say-to-spell | Model say-to-spell for EVERY schwa word. |
| Worksheet isn't in the lesson pack | Lesson type doesn't need one | Phonemic-awareness, review, and some assessment lessons don't have worksheets by design. Check the pack's prep checklist. |

More detailed troubleshooting: `reference/troubleshooting.html`

## FAQ

**Q: Can I skip Stage 1 if my child already knows letter sounds?**

A: No. Phonemic awareness (Stage 1 lessons 1-8) is oral — no letters involved. It builds the auditory foundation that spelling analysis depends on. Skipping causes problems in Stage 2+.

**Q: My child is older (Grade 2-3) and just starting. Where do they begin?**

A: Take the placement test (if available in your release), or start at Stage 1 Lesson 9 (first phonogram). Older kids move faster through the early stages — 5-6 lessons/week. Don't skip the assessments.

**Q: How is this different from the official Logic of English curriculum?**

A: This is a free, open-source adaptation. Scope:
- 248 lessons vs. official's 350-360 units (we compress multi-part lessons into single teaching sessions)
- Same 75 phonograms, 31 rules
- Same methodology (spelling analysis, say-to-spell, no sight words)
- Open-source generators, customizable, no DRM

**Q: Can I edit a lesson?**

A: Yes for personal use. For repo-wide changes, edit the generator script (see [docs/BUILD.md § Development Workflow](BUILD.md#development-workflow)).

**Q: Are these materials print-friendly?**

A: Yes — letter-size, 0.75" margins (0.5" for worksheets), grayscale-safe, font fallbacks for any printer.

**Q: Do I need internet to use the curriculum?**

A: No. The PDFs are static. The web game works offline (self-contained HTML). The audio MP3s are bundled.

**Q: What about kids with dyslexia or other learning differences?**

A: The systematic, multi-sensory approach (phonemic awareness → phonograms → spelling → reading) is research-aligned with dyslexia interventions. The pace is slower than typical curricula, which helps. See "Spelling drives reading" in [RESOURCES.md](../RESOURCES.md).
