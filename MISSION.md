# Mission: Teach Reading Through the Logic of English

## Why
Parents, tutors, and teachers need ready-to-use materials to teach children how to read using the structured literacy principles from Denise Eide's *Uncovering the Logic of English*. The existing curriculum document provides the blueprint; now we need the actual learning modules for children and quick-reference aids for the adults delivering the instruction. A child who learns these 106 tools reads independently, spells accurately, and never has to guess.

## Success looks like
- A parent or teacher can open any teacher aid and immediately know what to teach, in what order, with what words
- A child can work through a learning module and practice one specific skill with immediate feedback
- The materials progress from "I know no letters" to "I can read chapter books" in a clean, unbroken sequence
- Every module ties back to a specific phonogram or spelling rule — nothing is taught as a "sight word"

## Constraints
- All materials must work offline (printable or single HTML file per module)
- Teacher aids must be scannable in under 2 minutes during a lesson
- Children's modules must be completable in 15-20 minutes
- No assumption of commercial products — use DIY materials (index cards, whiteboard, sand tray)

## Out of scope
- Full Foundations/Essentials commercial curriculum replication (scripted lessons, game boards, readers)
- Video content production
- Non-English phonics
- Handwriting curriculum beyond basic letter formation cues
