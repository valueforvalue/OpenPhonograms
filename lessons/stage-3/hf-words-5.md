## Today's Words

### once

O says /wŭ/ — a rare case where O says /wŭ/. N says /n/. C says /s/ (Rule 1, before E). E is silent (Rule 12.9). Say-to-spell: /ōns/.

### two

TW says /tw/. O says /ö/. The W is doing double duty. Say-to-spell: /twō/.

### does

D says /d/. OE says /ŭ/ — unusual! S says /z/. Say-to-spell: /dōz/.

### any

A says /ĕ/ — unusual! N says /n/. Y says /ē/ at end (Rule 7). Say-to-spell: /ānē/.

### many

M says /m/. A says /ĕ/ (like 'any'). N says /n/. Y says /ē/. Say-to-spell: /mānē/.
