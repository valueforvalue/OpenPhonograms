## New Learning: Consonant + LE

### What Are Syllables?

A syllable is a word part with ONE vowel sound. Every syllable has exactly one vowel sound. When we read long words, we break them into syllables.

### How to Divide: Consonant + LE

When a word ends in a consonant + LE, the LE forms its own syllable. ta/ble, puz/zle, can/dle. The E is there because every syllable needs a vowel (Rule 12.4).
