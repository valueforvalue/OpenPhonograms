## New Learning: Silent E Reason 12.4

### The Reason

> **Every syllable must have a vowel. Silent E provides the vowel for the final syllable.**

### Why?

In words like 'little,' 'table,' and 'puzzle,' the final syllable (tle, ble, zle) needs a vowel. The E is that vowel! Without it, we'd have 'littl' — and every syllable needs a vowel.
