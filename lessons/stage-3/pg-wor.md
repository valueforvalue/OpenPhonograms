## New Learning: The Phonogram **wor**

<div class="phonogram">wor</div>

**wor** says 1 sound: /wer/

WOR is a special phonogram where the W changes the sound of OR. Instead of /or/, it says /wer/. Think: 'W+OR = /wer/' as in 'work' and 'world.'


| Sound | Example Words |
|-------|--------------|
| /wer/ | work, world, worm, worth |

---

## Spelling Analysis

| Word | Phonograms Used | Rules Applied | Say-to-Spell |
|------|----------------|---------------|--------------|
| work | wor (/wer/), k (/k/) | WOR — W changes OR | /werk/ |
| world | wor (/wer/), l (/l/), d (/d/) | WOR | /werld/ |
| worm | wor (/wer/), m (/m/) | WOR | /werm/ |

<details class="teacher-script">
<summary>📖 Phonogram wor — Teacher Script (click to expand)</summary>

> **Say:** "Today we are going to learn a new phonogram. A phonogram is a letter or group of letters that makes one or more sounds."

<div class="phonogram">wor</div>

> **Say:** "This is the phonogram **wor**." *(Point to the card.)* "It says /wer/."

> **Say:** "Listen to me say its sounds: /wer/. Now you say them."

> *(Child repeats.)*

> **Say:** "Good. Now let's write it. Watch me first." *(Demonstrate writing wor.)*

> **Say:** "Now you write **wor** three times. Say '/wer/' each time you write it."

> *(Child writes on whiteboard. Check formation.)*

> **Say:** "Every time you see this phonogram, you will try each of its sounds until the word makes sense. Let's practice with some words."



> **Say:** "What did you learn today?" *(Child responds: "The phonogram wor — it says /wer/.")*
