## New Learning: Silent E Reason 12.6

### The Reason

> **Silent E is added to very short content words to make them look 'complete.'**

### Why?

Very short words look odd in English. 'Pi,' 'ri,' 'aw' — these look incomplete. Adding a silent E makes them look like real English words: pie, rye, awe, owe, bye, dye.
