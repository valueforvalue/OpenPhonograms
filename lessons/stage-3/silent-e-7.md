## New Learning: Silent E Reason 12.7

### The Reason

> **Silent E clarifies that TH is voiced (/th/ as in 'this'), not unvoiced (/th/ as in 'thin').**

### Why?

At the end of a word, TH is usually unvoiced (bath, path). When TH is voiced, we add silent E to mark it: bathe, clothe, breathe, teethe.
