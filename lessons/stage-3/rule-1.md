## New Learning: Rule 1

### The Rule

> ****C** always softens to /s/ when followed by **E**, **I**, or **Y**. Otherwise, **C** says /k/.**

### Why This Rule Matters

C has two sounds: /k/ (hard) and /s/ (soft). When E, I, or Y follows C, it ALWAYS softens to /s/. When A, O, U, or a consonant follows, C says /k/. This is completely reliable — no exceptions!

### Examples

cent, city, cycle, face, ice, dance (C=/s/) · cat, cot, cup, clip, crab (C=/k/)
