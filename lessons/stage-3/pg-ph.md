## New Learning: The Phonogram **ph**

<div class="phonogram">ph</div>

**ph** says 1 sound: /f/

PH is a two-letter /f/ that comes from Greek. Most English words with PH are Greek in origin. It's like an ancient 'F'!


| Sound | Example Words |
|-------|--------------|
| /f/ | phone, graph, dolphin, elephant |

---

## Spelling Analysis

| Word | Phonograms Used | Rules Applied | Say-to-Spell |
|------|----------------|---------------|--------------|
| phone | ph (/f/), o (/ō/), n (/n/), e — silent E (12.1) | PH = /f/ (Greek) | /fōn/ |
| graph | g (/g/), r (/r/), a (/ă/), ph (/f/) | PH at end | /grăf/ |
| dolphin | d (/d/), o (/ŏ/), l (/l/), ph (/f/), i (/ĭ/), n (/n/) | PH in middle | dŏl-fĭn |
