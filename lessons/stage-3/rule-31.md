## New Learning: Rule 31

### The Rule

> **Any vowel may say the schwa sound /ə/ in an unstressed syllable.**

### Why This Rule Matters

Schwa is the most common vowel sound in English — the lazy /ə/ (like a tiny 'uh'). It happens in unstressed syllables and can be spelled by ANY vowel. This is why say-to-spell is so important!

### Examples

about (a=/ə/), seven (e=/ə/), pencil (i=/ə/), button (o=/ə/), circus (u=/ə/)
