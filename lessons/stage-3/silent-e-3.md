## New Learning: Silent E Reason 12.3

### The Reason

> **Silent E lets C say /s/ and G say /j/.**

### Why?

C says /s/ before E, and G may say /j/ before E. But what if the E needs to be silent? We add a silent E after the C or G, and another letter between them. In 'dance,' the C sees E (across the N) and says /s/. In 'change,' the G sees E and says /j/.
