## New Learning: The Phonogram **tch**

<div class="phonogram">tch</div>

**tch** says 1 sound: /ch/

TCH is a three-letter /ch/ used ONLY after a short vowel (or broad A in 'watch'). Compare: 'rich' (consonant before CH — no TCH) vs. 'catch' (short A — TCH).


> **Rule:** Rule 27: TCH is used only after a single vowel which says its short or broad sound.

| Sound | Example Words |
|-------|--------------|
| /ch/ | catch, watch, match, pitch |

<details class="teacher-script">
<summary>📖 Phonogram tch — Teacher Script (click to expand)</summary>

> **Say:** "Today we are going to learn a new phonogram. A phonogram is a letter or group of letters that makes one or more sounds."

<div class="phonogram">tch</div>

> **Say:** "This is the phonogram **tch**." *(Point to the card.)* "It says /ch/."

> **Say:** "Listen to me say its sounds: /ch/. Now you say them."

> *(Child repeats.)*

> **Say:** "Good. Now let's write it. Watch me first." *(Demonstrate writing tch.)*

> **Say:** "Now you write **tch** three times. Say '/ch/' each time you write it."

> *(Child writes on whiteboard. Check formation.)*

> **Say:** "Every time you see this phonogram, you will try each of its sounds until the word makes sense. Let's practice with some words."



> **Say:** "What did you learn today?" *(Child responds: "The phonogram tch — it says /ch/.")*
