## New Learning: The Phonogram **ew**

<div class="phonogram">ew</div>

**ew** says 2 sounds: /ü/ /ö/

EW says /ü/ (as in 'few') most of the time. Only 'sew' uses the /ö/ sound. EW is always at the end of a word or syllable.


| Sound | Example Words |
|-------|--------------|
| /ü/ | few, new, grew, chew |
| /ö/ | sew |

## Spelling Analysis

| Word | Phonograms Used | Rules Applied | Say-to-Spell |

| grew | g (/g/), r (/r/), ew (/ü/) | EW at end | /grü/ |

<details class="teacher-script">
<summary>📖 Phonogram ew — Teacher Script</summary>

> **Say:** "Today we are going to learn a new phonogram. A phonogram is a letter or group of letters that makes one or more sounds."

<div class="phonogram">ew</div>

> **Say:** "This is the phonogram **ew**." *(Point to the card.)* "It says /ü/ /ö/."

> **Say:** "Listen to me say its sounds: /ü/ /ö/. Now you say them."

> *(Child repeats.)*

> **Say:** "Good. Now let's write it. Watch me first." *(Demonstrate writing ew.)*

> **Say:** "Now you write **ew** three times. Say '/ü/ /ö/' each time you write it."

> *(Child writes on whiteboard. Check formation.)*

> **Say:** "Every time you see this phonogram, you will try each of its sounds until the word makes sense. Let's practice with some words."



> **Say:** "What did you learn today?" *(Child responds: "The phonogram ew — it says /ü/ /ö/.")*
