## Warm-Up: Speed Flash

---

## DGE or GE?

Adult says a word. Child decides: DGE or GE?

bridge (DGE — short i)
cage (GE — long a)
fudge (DGE — short u)
large (GE — consonant r before)
edge (DGE — short e)
huge (GE — long u)

---

## TCH or CH?

Same game for TCH/CH:

catch (TCH — short a)
inch (CH — consonant n before)
pitch (TCH — short i)
lunch (CH — consonant n before)
notch (TCH — short o)

---

## Build It

Write: bridge, edge, catch, watch, large, inch. Underline DGE/TCH/GE/CH.

---

## Spelling Challenge

Spell these words from dictation:

> bridge, catch, large, watch, edge, inch
