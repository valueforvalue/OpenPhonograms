## New Learning: The Phonogram **ei**

<div class="phonogram">ei</div>

**ei** says 3 sounds: /ē/ /ā/ /ī/

EI has three sounds: /ē/ (most common in Latin-based words), /ā/ (in a few words), /ī/ (rare — only a handful). It follows Rule 1/2 patterns with C and G.


| Sound | Example Words |
|-------|--------------|
| /ē/ | ceiling, receive, deceive |
| /ā/ | vein, rein, feign |
| /ī/ | feisty, heist, seismic |

## Spelling Analysis

| Word | Phonograms Used | Rules Applied | Say-to-Spell |
|------|----------------|---------------|--------------|
| ceiling | c (/s/), ei (/ē/), l (/l/), i (/ĭ/), ng (/ng/) | Rule 1: C=/s/ before EI | sē-lĭng |
| vein | v (/v/), ei (/ā/), n (/n/) | EI=/ā/ (rare) | /vān/ |
| feisty | f (/f/), ei (/ī/), s (/s/), t (/t/), y (/ē/) | EI=/ī/ (rare) | fī-stē |

<details class="teacher-script">
<summary>📖 Phonogram ei — Teacher Script (click to expand)</summary>

> **Say:** "Today we are going to learn a new phonogram. A phonogram is a letter or group of letters that makes one or more sounds."

<div class="phonogram">ei</div>

> **Say:** "This is the phonogram **ei**." *(Point to the card.)* "It says /ē/ /ā/ /ī/."

> **Say:** "Listen to me say its sounds: /ē/ /ā/ /ī/. Now you say them."

> *(Child repeats.)*

> **Say:** "Good. Now let's write it. Watch me first." *(Demonstrate writing ei.)*

> **Say:** "Now you write **ei** three times. Say '/ē/ /ā/ /ī/' each time you write it."

> *(Child writes on whiteboard. Check formation.)*

> **Say:** "Every time you see this phonogram, you will try each of its sounds until the word makes sense. Let's practice with some words."



> **Say:** "What did you learn today?" *(Child responds: "The phonogram ei — it says /ē/ /ā/ /ī/.")*
