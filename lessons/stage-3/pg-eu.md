## New Learning: The Phonogram **eu**

<div class="phonogram">eu</div>

**eu** says 2 sounds: /ü/ /ö/

EU says /ü/ in most words (neutral, feud). It appears in words of Greek and Latin origin. EU is never at the end of an English word.


| Sound | Example Words |
|-------|--------------|
| /ü/ | neutral, feud, eucalyptus |
| /ö/ | — |

<summary>📖 Phonogram eu — Teacher Script (click to expand)</summary>

> **Say:** "Today we are going to learn a new phonogram. A phonogram is a letter or group of letters that makes one or more sounds."

<div class="phonogram">eu</div>

> **Say:** "This is the phonogram **eu**." *(Point to the card.)* "It says /ü/ /ö/."

> **Say:** "Listen to me say its sounds: /ü/ /ö/. Now you say them."

> *(Child repeats.)*

> **Say:** "Good. Now let's write it. Watch me first." *(Demonstrate writing eu.)*

> **Say:** "Now you write **eu** three times. Say '/ü/ /ö/' each time you write it."

> *(Child writes on whiteboard. Check formation.)*

> **Say:** "Every time you see this phonogram, you will try each of its sounds until the word makes sense. Let's practice with some words."



> **Say:** "What did you learn today?" *(Child responds: "The phonogram eu — it says /ü/ /ö/.")*
