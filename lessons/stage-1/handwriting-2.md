## ## Straight-Start Letters

These letters all start with a straight line down. The letters are: **t, i, p, u, j, r, n, m, l, b, h, k, f** Letters



### How to Write Each Letter

### t
Start at the top line. Straight line down. Cross in the middle.

### i
Start at the midline. Straight line down. Dot at the top.

### p
Start at the midline. Straight line down below the baseline. Go back up and make a circle.

### u
Start at the midline. Curve down, then back up. Straight line down.

### j
Start at the midline. Straight down below baseline. Hook left. Dot at top.

### r
Start at the midline. Straight line down. Go back up and curve over.

### n
Start at the midline. Straight down. Go back up and make a hill.

### m
Start at the midline. Straight down. Go back up and make TWO hills.

### l
Start at the top line. Straight line down.

### b
Start at the top line. Straight line down. Go back up to midline and make a circle.

### h
Start at the top line. Straight line down. Go back up and make a hump.

### k
Start at the top line. Straight line down. From the middle, slant in then out.

### f
Start at the top line. Curve around and straight down. Cross in the middle.
