# Lesson 4: Blending Syllables

**Stage 1** · Lesson 4 · phonemic-awareness

---

## Warm-Up: Listening Game

> Adult: Say these directions. Child listens and follows along.

Now we'll blend bigger word parts called syllables. These aren't whole words by themselves, but when we put them together, they make a word!

---

## Activity 1: Clap the Parts

Adult says a word in slow syllables. Child blends them into one word.

Start with 2 syllables:
- kin ... der → (no, that's part of) kin-der-gar-ten → kindergarten
Try these 2-syllable words:
- ta ... ble → table
- pen ... cil → pencil
- pa ... per → paper
- kit ... ten → kitten
- hap ... py → happy
- win ... dow → window

---

## Activity 2: Three-Part Challenge

Now try 3 syllables!

- el ... e ... phant → elephant
- ba ... na ... na → banana
- to ... ma ... to → tomato
- bi ... cy ... cle → bicycle
- oc ... to ... pus → octopus
- um ... brel ... la → umbrella

---

## Activity 3: Syllable Count

Adult says a word at normal speed. Child claps once for each syllable.

1 clap: dog, cat, fish, sun, bed
2 claps: ta-ble, pen-cil, kit-ten
3 claps: el-e-phant, ba-na-na
4 claps: kin-der-gar-ten

---

## Let's Move!

March around the room! Each step = one syllable. Adult calls out words, child marches the syllables. 'El' (step) 'e' (step) 'phant' (step)!

---

## Quick Check

1. Clap 'water.' How many syllables? *(2: wa-ter)*
2. Clap 'butterfly.' How many? *(3: but-ter-fly)*
3. Say a word with 2 syllables. Say a different word with 3 syllables.
