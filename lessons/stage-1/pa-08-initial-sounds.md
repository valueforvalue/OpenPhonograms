# Lesson 8: First Sounds in Words

**Stage 1** · Lesson 8 · phonemic-awareness

---

## Warm-Up: Listening Game

> Adult: Say these directions. Child listens and follows along.

Let's practice hearing the FIRST sound in words. This is one of the most important reading skills!

---

## Activity 1: What's the First Sound?

Adult says a word. Child says just the first sound.

dog → /d/
cat → /k/
fish → /f/
sun → /s/
man → /m/
hat → /h/
top → /t/
run → /r/
bed → /b/
pig → /p/

Go faster once the child gets it!

---

## Activity 2: First Sound Match

Adult says 'I'm thinking of a word that starts with /b/.' Child guesses words that start with /b/ (bat, ball, big, bed, bug...).

Then switch — child picks a sound and adult guesses words.

Try: /s/ (sun, sit, sad, sip), /m/ (man, map, mat, mom), /t/ (top, tap, ten, tub)

---

## Activity 3: Odd One Out

Adult says three words. Two start with the same sound. One doesn't. Child finds the odd one.

- dog, cat, dad (cat — starts with /k/, not /d/)
- sun, sit, mop (mop — starts with /m/, not /s/)
- hat, bat, hop (bat — starts with /b/, not /h/)
- fan, dog, fish (dog — starts with /d/, not /f/)
- rug, red, big (big — starts with /b/, not /r/)

---

## Let's Move!

Scavenger hunt! Find something in the room that starts with /b/ (book?), /k/ (cup?), /s/ (sock?). Bring it back to the adult and say its first sound!

---

## Quick Check

1. What's the first sound in 'mom'? *(/m/)*
2. Which word doesn't belong: hat, dog, hop? *(dog — starts with /d/, not /h/)*
3. Name three things that start with /s/.
