## New Learning: **-er/-or**

### What Is Suffix?

-ER and -OR both mean 'one who' or 'that which does something.'

### Suffix Means: "one who, that which"

### Words with Suffix

| Word | Suffix + Root | Meaning |
|------|---|------||---------|
| teach er → teacher | — | — |
| act or → actor | — | — |
| bake er → baker | — | — |
| sail or → sailor | — | — |
