## New Learning: **-er/-or**

### What Is Prefix?

-ER and -OR both mean 'one who' or 'that which does something.'

### Prefix Means: "one who, that which"

### Words with Prefix

| Word | Prefix + Root | Meaning |
|------|------||---------|
| teach er → teacher | — | — |
| act or → actor | — | — |
| bake er → baker | — | — |
| sail or → sailor | — | — |
