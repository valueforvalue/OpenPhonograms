## New Learning: **-less/-ous**

### What Is Suffix?

-LESS means 'without.' -OUS means 'full of' or 'having the quality of.'

### Suffix Means: "without, full of"

### Words with Suffix

| Word | Suffix + Root | Meaning |
|------|---|------||---------|
| hope less → hopeless | — | — |
| danger ous → dangerous | — | — |
| fear less → fearless | — | — |
| joy ous → joyous | — | — |
