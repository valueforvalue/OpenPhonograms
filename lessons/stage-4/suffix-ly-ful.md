## New Learning: **-tion/-sion**

### What Is Suffix?

-TION and -SION mean 'the act of' or 'state of.' They turn verbs into nouns.

### Suffix Means: "act of, state of"

### Words with Suffix

| Word | Suffix + Root | Meaning |
|------|---|------||---------|
| act tion → action | — | — |
| divi sion → division | — | — |
| collect tion → collection | — | — |
| deci sion → decision | — | — |
