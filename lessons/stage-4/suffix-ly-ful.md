## New Learning: **-ly/-ful**

### What Is Suffix?

-LY means 'in a ___ way' (adverbs). -FUL means 'full of.'

### Suffix Means: "in a way, full of"

### Words with Suffix

| Word | Suffix + Root | Meaning |
|------|---|------||---------|
| quick ly → quickly | — | — |
| hope ful → hopeful | — | — |
| slow ly → slowly | — | — |
| joy ful → joyful | — | — |
