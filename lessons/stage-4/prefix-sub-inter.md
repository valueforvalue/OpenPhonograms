## New Learning: **un-**

### What Is Prefix?

UN- means 'not' or 'opposite.' RE- means 'again' or 'back.'

### Prefix Means: "not, opposite of"

### Words with Prefix

| Word | Prefix + Root | Meaning |
|------|------||---------|
| un do → undo | — | — |
| re do → redo | — | — |
| un tie → untie | — | — |
| re turn → return | — | — |
