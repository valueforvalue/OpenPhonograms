## New Learning: **pre-/pro-**

### What Is Prefix?

PRE- means 'before.' PRO- means 'forward' or 'for.'

### Prefix Means: "before, forward"

### Words with Prefix

| Word | Prefix + Root | Meaning |
|------|------||---------|
| pre view → preview | — | — |
| pro ceed → proceed | — | — |
| pre pay → prepay | — | — |
| pro pel → propel | — | — |
