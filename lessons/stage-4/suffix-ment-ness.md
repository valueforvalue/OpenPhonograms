## New Learning: **-ment/-ness**

### What Is Suffix?

-MENT means 'the result of' or 'state of.' -NESS means 'the quality of being.'

### Suffix Means: "state of, quality of"

### Words with Suffix

| Word | Suffix + Root | Meaning |
|------|---|------||---------|
| enjoy ment → enjoyment | — | — |
| dark ness → darkness | — | — |
| pay ment → payment | — | — |
