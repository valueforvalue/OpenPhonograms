📖 Lesson 1: Your First Phonograms

Today you'll learn three phonograms: a, d, and g. A phonogram is a letter (or group of letters) that makes one or more sounds.

Step 1

Meet the phonogram a

Click the big letter below. Say each sound out loud!

a

a says three sounds:

/ă/ /ā/ /ä/

/ă/ like in at · /ā/ like in nation · /ä/ like in father

Now you write it! Get your whiteboard or paper. Write a big a while saying /ă/, /ā/, /ä/. Do this three times.

Meet the phonogram d

d

/d/

/d/ like in dog

Meet the phonogram g

g

g says two sounds:

Step 5

Flash Card Practice

Grown-up: flash these three cards to the child. Child says ALL sounds for each phonogram. Aim for under 2 seconds per card!

a → /ă/ /ā/ /ä/ d → /d/ g → /g/ /j/

Shuffle the order! Mix them up. See if the child can say all sounds no matter which card comes first.

Step 6

Blending Game: Put Sounds Together

Grown-up says these sounds slowly. Child blends them into a word!

/d/ /ă/ /d/

dad! Did you get it?
