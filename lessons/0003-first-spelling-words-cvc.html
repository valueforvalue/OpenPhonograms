Prerequisites: Child knows all sounds for a, c, d, g, o, qu, s, t, i, p, u, j, r, n, m, e, l, b

Warm-up

Phonogram Flash Review

Flash these cards. Child says ALL sounds. Under 2 seconds each!

a /ă/ /ā/ /ä/ c /k/ /s/ t /t/ d /d/ g /g/ /j/ o /ŏ/ /ō/ /ö/ s /s/ /z/ p /p/ n /n/ m /m/
Phonemic Awareness

Blending Drill

Grown-up says the sounds. Child says the word!

/k/ /ă/ /t/ → cat!

/d/ /ă/ /d/ → dad!

/s/ /ă/ /d/ → sad!

Spelling Analysis

Word 1: cat

1. Hear & Say: The word is cat. A cat is an animal that says meow. Say it.

2. Segment: What sounds do you hear in "cat"?

1 /k/   1 /ă/   1 /t/

Grown-up holds up 1 finger for each sound — single letters!

3. Write: Now write "cat" on your whiteboard. Sound it out as you write!

✏️ Write cat here

4. Analyze:

c a t
c → /k/ (first sound)
a → /ă/ (first sound, short vowel)
t → /t/
All single-letter phonograms! No special rules needed.

5. Read: /k/ /ă/ /t/ → cat!
