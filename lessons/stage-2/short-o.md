## New Learning: The Short Sound of **O**

### Meet /ŏ/

**O** has multiple sounds. The short sound of **o** is /ŏ/.

The short sound of O is /ŏ/ — like the first sound in 'octopus.' Your mouth makes a round circle. Imagine you're surprised: 'O!' but shorter. Say it: /ŏ/.

<div class="phonogram">o</div>

### The Breve Mark ˘

When o says its short sound /ŏ/, we can mark it with a **breve** (˘) — a little smile above the letter. This helps us remember it's the short sound.

> ō  = long &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; ŏ = short (breve)
