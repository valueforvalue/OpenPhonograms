## New Learning: The Short Sound of **U**

### Meet /ŭ/

**U** has multiple sounds. The short sound of **u** is /ŭ/.

The short sound of U is /ŭ/ — like the first sound in 'up' or 'umbrella.' Your mouth is relaxed and slightly open. Point UP when you say /ŭ/!

<div class="phonogram">u</div>

### The Breve Mark ˘

When u says its short sound /ŭ/, we can mark it with a **breve** (˘) — a little smile above the letter. This helps us remember it's the short sound.

> ū  = long &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; ŭ = short (breve)
