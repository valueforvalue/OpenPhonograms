## New Learning: The Short Sound of **A**

### Meet /ă/

**A** has multiple sounds. The short sound of **a** is /ă/.

The short sound of A is /ă/ — like the first sound in 'apple.' Your mouth is open, your tongue is low. Try singing it: /ăăăă/. You can't hold it as long as /ā/, can you? That's why it's called 'short.'

<div class="phonogram">a</div>

### The Breve Mark ˘

When a says its short sound /ă/, we can mark it with a **breve** (˘) — a little smile above the letter. This helps us remember it's the short sound.

> ā  = long &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; ă = short (breve)
