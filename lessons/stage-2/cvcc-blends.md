## New Learning: Words That End with Two Consonants

### What We're Building Today

Consonant blends can also come at the END of words. Listen carefully for both final sounds — they can be tricky! /h/ /ă/ /n/ /d/ → hand.

> **Listen carefully:** The last two sounds are separate — don't skip one! 'hand' has 4 sounds: /h/ /ă/ /n/ /d/.
