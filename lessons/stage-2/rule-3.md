## New Learning: Rule 3

### The Rule

> **English words do not end in **I**, **U**, **V**, or **J**.**

### Why This Rule Exists

This rule explains so many spellings! Because words cannot end in I, we use Y instead (boy, day). Because words cannot end in U, we use W at the end (saw, new). Because words cannot end in V, we add silent E (have, give). This is one of the most important spelling rules.

### Examples

have, give, blue (silent E protects V/U) · boy, day, play (Y replaces I) · saw, law (W replaces U)
