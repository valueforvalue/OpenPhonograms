## New Learning: Blends at BOTH Ends!

### What We're Building Today

The ultimate blending challenge — words with consonant blends at the beginning AND the end. Five sounds to blend! /p/ /l/ /ă/ /n/ /t/ → plant.

> **Five sounds!** These are the longest one-syllable words: /s/+/t/+/ă/+/m/+/p/ = stamp. Count them on your fingers.
