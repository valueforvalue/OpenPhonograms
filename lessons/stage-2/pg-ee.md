## New Learning: The Phonogram **ee**

### What Is **ee**?

<div class="phonogram">ee</div>

**ee** says 1 sound: /ē/

Double E always says /ē/. It's one of the most reliable phonograms in English!


### Sound Table

| Sound | Example Words |
|-------|--------------|
| /ē/ | see, green, feet, tree |

---

## Spelling Analysis

| Word | Phonograms Used | Rules Applied | Say-to-Spell |
|------|----------------|---------------|--------------|
| see | s (/s/), ee (/ē/) | Double E always /ē/ | /sē/ |
| feet | f (/f/), ee (/ē/), t (/t/) | Double E always /ē/ | /fēt/ |
| green | g (/g/), r (/r/), ee (/ē/), n (/n/) | Double E always /ē/ | /grēn/ |
