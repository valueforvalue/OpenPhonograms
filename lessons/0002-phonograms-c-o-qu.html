📖 Lesson 2: Phonograms c, o, qu

Let's review first! Flash your a, d, g cards. Then learn three new phonograms.

Review

Flash Cards: a, d, g

Grown-up: show each card. Child says ALL sounds fast. If slow, practice that one before moving on.

a → /ă/ /ā/ /ä/ d → /d/ g → /g/ /j/

Step 1

Meet the phonogram c

c

c says two sounds:

/k/ /s/

/k/ like in cat · /s/ like in cent

Important! c always says /k/ unless followed by e, i, or y — then it says /s/. Don't worry about this rule yet. Just know both sounds!

Write c three times while saying /k/, /s/.

Step 2

Meet the phonogram o

o

o says four sounds:

/ŏ/ /ō/ /ö/ /ŭ/

/ŏ/ like odd · /ō/ like go · /ö/ like to · /ŭ/ like son

Write o three times while saying all four sounds.

✏️ Write o here
Step 3

Meet the phonogram qu

qu

qu is special — it's two letters that make ONE sound together:

/kw/

Rule 11: Q always needs a U. The U in QU is NOT a vowel — it's part of the consonant team! So qu is always together.

Write qu three times while saying /kw/.

✏️ Write qu here
Step 4

Quiz Time!

1. What two sounds does c say?

/k/ (like cat) and /s/ (like cent)

2. What are the four sounds of o?

/ŏ/ /ō/ /ö/ /ŭ/ — short, long, broad, and schwa!

3. What sound does qu make? Why are q and u always together?

/kw/ — because Q always needs U (Rule 11)!
Step 5

Blending Practice

Grown-up says the sounds. Child blends to a word!

/k/ /ă/ /t/

cat!

/d/ /ŏ/ /g/

dog!
