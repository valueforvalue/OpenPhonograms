## Story

<div class="reader-page">

<div class="reader-text">

## Think About It

1. Why do animals avoid Stella? (Her powerful scent.) 2. How does Stella save the rabbits? (She scares away a fox.) 3. Find words with suffixes. (protection, powerful, dangerous, loneliness)
