## After Reading

<div class="comprehension">

**Retell the Story**

What does the sun do in the day? What is in the sky at night?

**Find the Sounds**

Circle every **s** in the story.
Circle every **n** in the story.

**Draw It**

Draw the sun in the sky. Draw the stars at night.
