## After Reading

<div class="comprehension">

**Retell the Story**

Where is the pig? What can the pig do?

**Find the Sounds**

Circle every **p** in the story.
Circle every **s** in the story.

**Draw It**

Draw the pig in the pen. What else is in the pen?
