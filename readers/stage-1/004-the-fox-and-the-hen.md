## After Reading

<div class="comprehension">

**Retell the Story**

What does the fox want? How does the hen stay safe?

**Find the Sounds**

Circle every **f** in the story.
Circle every **h** in the story.

**Draw It**

Draw the fox and the hen. Where is the box?
