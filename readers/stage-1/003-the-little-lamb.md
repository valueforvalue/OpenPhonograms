## After Reading

<div class="comprehension">

**Retell the Story**

What is the lamb's name? What does the lamb do?

**Find the Sounds**

Circle every **l** in the story.
Circle every **m** in the story.

**Draw It**

Draw the little lamb in the meadow. What is nearby?
