yăk &nbsp;&nbsp; zĕ &nbsp;&nbsp; bră &nbsp;&nbsp; (yes!) &nbsp;&nbsp; zĕ-bră

<div class="reader-page">

<div class="reader-text">

A yak and a zebra met on the plain.

The yak said, "I have a long, soft coat."

The zebra said, "I have stripes!"

</div>

## Page 2

<div class="reader-page">

<div class="reader-text">

## Page 3

<div class="reader-page">

<div class="reader-text">

The yak drank from a jug of cold water.

The zebra drank, too.

The sun set. The sky turned red.

The yak and the zebra went home.

## After Reading

<div class="comprehension">

**Retell the Story**

What do the yak and the zebra each look like? What do they do together?

**Find the Sounds**

Circle every **y** in the story.
Circle every **z** in the story.
Can you find at least one of every phonogram a–z?

**Draw It**

Draw the yak and the zebra in their home on the plain.

</div>
