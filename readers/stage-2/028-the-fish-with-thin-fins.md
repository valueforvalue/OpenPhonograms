## Think About It

1. Why does Finn swim to the creek? (He is curious.) 2. What happens to Finn? (He gets stuck behind a stick.) 3. How does Finn get back to the pond? (The duck pulls the stick out.) 4. Find a CK word in the story. (duck, back, stick, rock, stuck).
