## Think About It

1. What does Gus see at the start? (A ship with a white sail.) 2. What helps the ship find safety? (Gus the bird sees a green light.) 3. How does the group thank Gus? (They pat Gus.) 4. Find an SH word in the story. (ship, lash, sharp).
