## Page 2

<div class="reader-page">

<div class="reader-text">

Fred can hop.

Hop, hop, hop!

Fred hops on the rock.

Fred hops in the pond.

Splash!

</div>

<div class="reader-sidebar">

### Spelling Aid

**Review:** ck

**Sounds:** /k/ after short vowel

**Rule 26:** CK after a single short vowel.

![Fred hopping](images/illustrations/fred-frog-cake.png)

</div>

</div>



## Page 3

<div class="reader-page">

<div class="reader-text">

Fred sees a fish.

The fish is big.

"Hi, Fish!" says Fred.

The fish swims.

Fred sits on the rock.

Fred and Fish are pals.

</div>

<div class="reader-sidebar">

### Spelling Aid

**Review:** sh, th, ee

**sh** = /sh/ (fish, ship)

**th** = /th/ (this, that)

**ee** = /ē/ (see, green)

![Fred and the fish](images/animals/fish.png)

</div>

</div>



## Page 4

<div class="reader-page">

<div class="reader-text">

The sun sets.

Fred is glad.

Fred has a pal.

Fred the frog is not sad.

Fred is a glad frog!

The end.
