# Phonogram Go Fish

**Instructions:** Print 4 copies of each card sheet. Cut out cards. Deal 5 cards to each player. Players ask: "Do you have the phonogram that says /ă/?" If yes, take the card and make a pair. Most pairs wins.

---

## Deck 1: Single-Letter Phonograms (Print 4 copies)

Cut along lines. Each card shows the phonogram.

```
| a | b | c | d | e | f | g | h | i | j |
| k | l | m | n | o | p | qu | r | s | t |
| u | v | w | x | y | z |
```
