# Phonogram Bingo

**Instructions:** Cut out the calling cards. Each player picks a bingo board. Adult calls a SOUND. Player marks the phonogram that makes that sound. First to get 3 in a row wins!

---

## Calling Cards (Cut Out)

Cut along lines. Adult draws one card and reads the sound aloud.

```
| /ă/ /ā/ /ä/ | /b/ | /k/ /s/ | /d/ | /ĕ/ /ē/ |
| /f/ | /g/ /j/ | /h/ | /ĭ/ /ī/ /ē/ | /j/ |
| /k/ | /l/ | /m/ | /n/ | /ŏ/ /ō/ /ö/ |
| /p/ | /kw/ | /r/ | /s/ /z/ | /t/ |
| /ŭ/ /ū/ /ö/ | /v/ | /w/ | /ks/ /z/ | /y/ /ĭ/ /ī/ /ē/ |
| /z/ | /sh/ | /th/ | /k/ (ck) | /ē/ (ee) |
| /ng/ | /är/ | /or/ | /er/ | /oi/ |
```

## Bingo Board 1

| a | sh | ee | or |
| b | th | ng | oi |
| c | ck | ar | er |

## Bingo Board 2

| d | ch | oo | ir |
| e | wh | ed | ur |
| f | ea | igh | oa |

## Bingo Board 3

| g | ow | aw | ear |
| h | ou | au | ai |
| i | oy | ay | oa |

---

**For more boards:** Each player writes 9 phonograms from the calling cards in a 3×3 grid on their whiteboard. New board every game!
