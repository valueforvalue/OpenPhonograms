# Reading Instruction Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: _Uncovering the Logic of English_ — Denise Eide](https://logicofenglish.com/products/uncovering-the-logic-of-english)
  The source text. 75 phonograms, 31 spelling rules, 44 phonemes. Use for: every rule, every phonogram definition, the spelling analysis methodology.

- [Logic of English — Spelling Rules (Official)](https://logicofenglish.com/pages/spelling-rules)
  Complete up-to-date list of all 31 rules with examples. Use for: verifying rule wording, finding example words.

- [Logic of English — Spelling Analysis (Official)](https://logicofenglish.com/pages/spelling-analysis)
  The core teaching routine: hear, segment, write, analyze, read. Use for: teacher aid methodology sections.

- [Foundations Scope & Sequence PDF](https://loe-assets.logicofenglish.com/downloads/foundations-a-b-c-d-ss.pdf)
  Official teaching order for phonograms and rules. Use for: verifying our module progression matches evidence-based sequence.

- [Interactive Phonogram Chart](https://logicofenglish.com/pages/phonogram-chart)
  Audio reference for all 75 phonogram sounds. Use for: verifying sound order and pronunciation.

- Campbell Creates Readers — [Book Review](https://www.campbellcreatesreaders.com/blog/book-review-understanding-the-logic-of-english)
  Practitioner perspective from a K-5 reading specialist. Use for: understanding what teachers actually need in aids.

- Ouellette, G., Martin-Chang, S., & Rossi, M. (2017). "Learning From Our Mistakes: Improvements in Spelling Lead to Gains in Reading Speed." _Scientific Studies of Reading_, 21(4), 350-357.
  Evidence that spelling instruction drives reading gains. Use for: justifying the spelling-analysis-first approach.
